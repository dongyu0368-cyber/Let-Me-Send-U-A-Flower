English

The sunlight slants gently through the window,

spilling over a faint loneliness.

Sitting alone in the corner,

letting my thoughts drift into emptiness.

Scenes flicker before my eyes,

so many trivial, tangled details.

What was it all for, this endless struggle?

Yet somehow, I cannot escape it.

Let me send you a flower — I have missed you for so long.

Let me send you a flower — the longing remains unchanged.

My heart feels like a parcel left anywhere,

scattered in the corners of the room.

Whether to face it or to walk away — I thought I understood.

Perhaps I should leave, change my mood, see a different view.

I think I need to find a reason.

I think I need the courage to cry without restraint.

Only then do I realize — you have always been there.

Let me send you a flower — I have missed you for so long.

Let me send you a flower — the longing still lingers.
